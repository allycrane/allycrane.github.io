BTU Lab

A Community Makerspace

The BTU Lab is an inclusive and eclectic community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of art, design, engineering and architecture, while supporting Creative Technology & Design academic programs. BTU is a general workspace open to all students at CU Boulder, offering access and training to a range of tools for 3D printing, woodworking, laser cutting, electronics prototyping, textiles/sewing and much more.
